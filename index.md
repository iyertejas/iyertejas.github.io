# Tejas Iyer

Postdoctoral Fellow, Weierstrass Institute for Applied Analysis and Stochastics (WIAS), Berlin
{: .lede}

I am a mathematician working in probability theory. This involves the rigorous analysis of random systems arising in diverse contexts, including learning algorithms, complex networks and the structure of biological populations. A key aspect of my research is understanding tipping points in such systems, where a small change in parameters leads to dramatic large-scale consequences.

Please consult my [research](research.html) page for more details (including simulations!).

I am a member of the Interacting Random Systems group at WIAS, within the DFG priority programme [SPP 2265 Random Geometric Systems](https://spp2265.wias-berlin.de/project.php?projectID=25), and I lecture at TU Berlin. I completed my PhD at the University of Birmingham in 2021, supervised by [Nikolaos Fountoulakis](http://web.mat.bham.ac.uk/N.Fountoulakis/) and co-supervised by [Richard Mycroft](http://web.mat.bham.ac.uk/~mycroftr/). Before that, I graduated from the Bachelor of Philosophy (Science) program at the Australian National University with First Class Honours in Mathematics, supervised by [Brendan McKay](http://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/) and co-supervised by [Kim Morrison](https://tqft.net/).
