1. Lai–Robbins implies that the minimax regret \(R_n^*\) grows like \(\log n\).

False. Lai–Robbins is about a fixed environment as \(n \to \infty\), and for Gaussian arms its constant \(\sum_a 2\sigma^2/\Delta_a\) blows up as the gaps shrink. For a fixed horizon, gaps of order \(\sqrt{K/n}\) give \(R_n^* \ge (\sigma/27)\sqrt{(K-1)n}\) for Gaussian arms (Remark 4.5(1)).

2. In the proof of Theorem 4.3, the arm whose law is changed can be fixed in advance, say arm 2.

False. It is an arm \(a' \ge 2\) with \(\mathbb E_\nu[N_{a'}(n)] \le n/(K-1)\), and which arm this is depends on \(\pi\). A policy that pulls arm 2 often would notice a change there: \(\mathbb E_\nu[N_2(n)]\,\mathrm{KL}\) could be of order \(n\).

3. In the proof of Theorem 4.3, the two environments depend on the horizon \(n\).

True. \(\Delta\) is chosen depending on \(n\). For each \(n\), \(\nu\) and \(\nu'\) are still ordinary SBMs, fixed for the whole run.

4. \(d_a \ge d^{\Delta_a}\) for every arm \(a\) with \(\Delta_a > 0\).

True. \(d_a\) is the same infimum as \(d^{\Delta_a}\) (Definition 4.2), with \(\mu = \mu_a\) fixed instead of free. It can be several times larger (Exercise 4.1).

5. \(\mathrm{UCB}(1/n^2)\) has worst-case regret of order \(\sqrt{Kn}\).

False. Corollary 4.7 gives \(8\sigma\sqrt{Kn\log n} + 3\sum_a \Delta_a\). The extra \(\sqrt{\log n}\) comes from \(\delta = 1/n^2\); MOSS removes it.

6. To use Lemma 4.6, the policy must know the threshold \(\eta\).

False. \(\eta\) appears only in the proof. The lemma turns any pull-count bound of the form \(A + B/\Delta_a^2\) into a worst-case bound.

7. If the means of \(\mathcal M\) form an interval, then \(R_n^*(\mathcal M^K)\) grows at least like \(\sqrt n\).

False. Theorem 4.3 also needs \(d^{2\Delta} < \infty\). For \(\mathcal M = \{\mathrm{Uniform}[m, m+1] : m \in [0,1]\}\), every \(d^\eta\) is infinite, and some policy has regret at most \(5(K-1)\) for every \(n\) (Exercise 4.5).
